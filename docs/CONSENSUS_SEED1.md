# Consensus arm, second training seed (seed 1), trained on Lambda Cloud

The case-level group-consensus arm (`configs/grpo_deferdx.yaml`, the published mechanism kept as a comparator) has one
training seed in the paper. This is its second, proposed in [EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md) ("Second seeds of the control and
the consensus arm") and trained on a rented GPU while the shared GPU trained the control's second seed.

**Status: trained (150 steps), not yet evaluated on the held-out suite.** Every number below is from the trainer's own
log: per-step training batches and the in-training dev evaluation (212 dev cases, one sample each). None of it is
comparable with the held-out tables in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) until `deferdx eval-suite` has run on this adapter.

## Summary

- **Ran to completion:** 150 of 150 steps in one attempt, no crashes or restarts (Oct 13, 14:27 UTC to Oct 14, 08:15 UTC; 17.1 h of steps plus 0.63 h of dev evaluation).
- **Cost:** $37.86 for the run (1x A100-SXM4-40GB at $1.99/h, 19.0 h including setup and the smoke test); $39.42 including two earlier aborted launches and one unused H100.
- **Dev results improved up to step 125, then fell back:** step 125 has the best dev accuracy, AURC, Brier score and invalid rate; step 100 the best calibration (ECE 0.032). The final adapter (step 150) is worse than both on accuracy on answered cases (0.899 vs 0.949 to 0.951), Brier score (0.108 vs 0.062 to 0.071) and deferral of OTHER cases (0.531 vs 0.688).
- **Late drift:** token entropy rose from 0.31 (steps 1 to 50) to 1.36 at step 150 (peak 1.64, step 144), steepest after step 125; the last step had 11.5% invalid episodes.
- **Open decision (PI):** the plan evaluates `final`, as for seed 0. Evaluating step 125 or step 100 instead would select a checkpoint on dev results, a different rule from seed 0's.

## Observations

- **Step time** averaged 507 s over steps 1-50 on the A100, close to the shared GPU's ~520 s for the seed-0 run, then fell to ~300 s in the last 50 steps as fewer groups had reward spread (`kept_groups`) and episodes used fewer tests.
- **Dev results** were flat to step 50 (within about one standard error of the step-3 values; ≈ 0.03 for accuracy on 212 cases), then improved steadily: accuracy on answered cases 0.871 to 0.951 (step 100), AURC 0.083 to 0.034 (step 125), tests per case 1.72 to 1.10 (step 125), OTHER deferral 0.344 to 0.688.
- **Entropy** stayed at ~0.31 through step 50 and rose once the 4 PPO mini-batches began at step 51 (0.40 mean over steps 51-75, 0.6-0.8 over 76-125). Accuracy and calibration kept improving while it rose, until about step 125. Engine mismatch stayed between 0.018 and 0.028 throughout, and the largest gradient norm (0.144, step 146) was about 7x below the 1.0 clip.
- **After step 125** entropy climbed faster (0.86 at step 125, 1.36 at step 150), and the final dev evaluation fell back on accuracy on answered cases, Brier score and OTHER deferral. Whether seed 0 showed the same late rise is not checked here: its training log is on the shared GPU.
